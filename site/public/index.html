Why Lowtide

A balance is a snapshot.
Your month is a tide.

The number in your bank app says nothing about next Tuesday, when rent and the card bill land two days before payday. Lowtide looks ahead for you.

Paychecks that change

Hours vary, so does pay. Lowtide reads your timesheets and learns what a normal week looks like. Extra hours stay extra.

Bills that don't

Rent, phone, insurance and the card land on fixed days. Every one of them is on the calendar before it hits.

A floor you never touch

Pick a reserve. Every number Lowtide shows you already keeps it out of reach, from the day it starts.

How it works

Three steps, then it runs itself.

  1. 1

    Bring what you already have

    Bank alert emails flow in twice a day; statements and CSVs drop in when you have them. Timesheets sync on their own. No bank password, ever.

  2. 2

    It forecasts every day

    Hours become paychecks on your real pay dates. Bills, budgets, trips and breaks go on the same timeline, day by day.

  3. 3

    You get one number

    What's safe to spend, every morning at 8 as a notification, with the low tide behind it whenever you open the app.

Features

Everything your month needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

The low tide, before it comes.

A day-by-day forecast to the end of next month: paydays, bills, the tightest day and your reserve on one chart. Budgets fill as you spend, so you see a problem while there is still time to change course.

  • Safe to spend, and how much that is per day
  • Whether next month is covered, job by job
  • The next 14 days of bills and paychecks

Twelve months ahead, always.

A rolling forecast of every month-end, savings goals filled in priority order, and how much room each trip has without breaking the reserve.

  • Goals with what you have now and what you'll have by the date
  • The extra saving a month that closes any gap
  • The assumptions behind it, written in plain words

Privacy and security

Your money.
Not your password.

No bank logins

Lowtide never asks for a bank password. Data comes from alert emails, files you import and numbers you type.

Passkeys only

Sign in with Face ID or Touch ID. There is no password to steal, phish or reuse.

Checked at the door

A bank email only counts when Gmail's DMARC check passes for that bank's exact domain.

Open and reviewed

Every line is on GitHub, with a threat model and two security reviews written up.

Read the security write-up →

Under the hood

Small, fast and free to run.

React on the front, a Cloudflare Worker with D1 behind it, Web Push written from the RFCs, and a forecasting engine in plain TypeScript, with a fixture test for every pay rule. It fits in the Workers free plan's 10 ms of CPU per request.
